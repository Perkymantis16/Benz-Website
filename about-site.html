Welcome to Benz Performance! Here you will not only learn what the imspiration behind this page was, but a little about me to. My name is Jake Crabtree, the creator of this website. I am a highschool student, and what inspired me to make Benz Performance was working on cars with my dad. I've always enjoyed working on Mercedes engines and the cosmetic look of them. And one thing I've always found difficult is that there is not a singular website dedicated to Mercedes-Benz and Mercedes-AMG aftermarket parts. So With me being enrolled into a coding class, I figured, why not try and make one. To as the themes of the website theres a reason behind that aswell. First off, the black background. The reason that the back ground is black is for two reasons. First reason, I myself hate reading black letter off of white screens or paper because at some point my eyes get strained so I went with the black to take strain off the eyes. Second reason is because when you think of Mercedes you probably think of them painted black. It's one of their most popular colors. As for the letters being silver. It is perhaps the most meaningful reason of all. The letters are silver, not exactly silver but it's silver. That's because Silver became legendary in the 1930s, when Mercedes race cars earned the name “Silver Arrows.” According to historical accounts, Mercedes’ 1934 W25 Grand Prix car appeared in bare metal aluminum, creating the striking silver look that became synonymous with their racing dominance. Another widely told version says the team stripped the white paint off the car to meet the 750‑kg weight limit, revealing the raw silver body underneath — and the car won, cementing the color as iconic. The Silver Arrow identity undeniably began with those 1930s race cars and has continued through modern Mercedes‑AMG Formula 1 machines. I hope you enjoyed leanering more about Benz Performance.
